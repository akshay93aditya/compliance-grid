# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Compliance Grid is a community-extending project. We pledge to make participation in our community a welcoming experience for everyone — regardless of background, experience level, identity, or any of the categories you'd expect to find in a longer document.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and constructive in code review.
- Welcoming new contributors — especially those adding their first recipe or yaml entry.
- Focusing on what's best for the community and the public knowledge graph.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.
- Acknowledging that compliance is a domain where mistakes have real consequences for real businesses, and treating every contribution with that seriousness.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Personal attacks, harassment, or insulting comments.
- Public or private harassment of community members.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Demanding free work or attempting to coerce maintainers into specific decisions.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Reports of unacceptable behaviour can be sent privately to the maintainer at the address configured in `COMPLIANCE_GRID_CONTACT_EMAIL` for this deployment, or via a private GitHub Security Advisory.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer team is obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.

Enforcement actions may include warnings, temporary bans, or permanent bans from the project — proportionate to the conduct.
